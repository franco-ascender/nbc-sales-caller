# 04 Source Catalog: Lead Engine (NBC Sales)

Consolidated, machine readable catalog of every data source, endpoint, file and vendor named in the seven research modules (Groups A, B, C, D, E, F and J, G, H, I). Research date of the underlying modules: 2026-09-19. This catalog adds nothing that is not in the modules: no URL was completed, guessed or re verified. Where a module gives an endpoint string, it is reproduced exactly; where it gives only a host or page, only that is reproduced.

**Column key.** Source name; State or scope; Access (exact URL, endpoint, dataset id, SFTP host, file name, or "per record only"); What it names (person owner, qualifier, entity, director); Phone field (yes person level, yes business level, none); Email; Row count or size; Update cadence; Cost; Legal or terms note; Tag (VERIFIED, REPORTED, UNKNOWN, as the module tagged it); Recipe hint.

**Recipe hint key.** C = register carries a phone, so verify only (line type, connected, DNC, litigator). B = name only, so needs an address plus a cell append. D = register phone versus Maps phone contrast (Bucket 2 versus 3 logic). A = Maps first (discovery is the contact source). AVOID = prohibited, restricted, or not worth the money; the reason is in the row.

**Socrata conventions used across the catalog.** SODA JSON endpoints follow `https://<domain>/resource/<dataset id>.json`; page with `$limit=50000&$offset=N`; send `X-App-Token: <token>`; long `$where` clauses go through `$query` or a POST body because the research proxy rejected URLs over roughly 300 characters and silently dropped parenthesised OR lists on data.ny.gov and data.texas.gov.

## Known traps (implement as assertions in the loaders)

- **TX TDLR owner_telephone mirrors business_telephone.** In 7358-krk7 a SoQL count of rows where owner_telephone differs from business_telephone returned exactly zero on Electrical Contractor rows, and the same mirror holds on cosmetology establishments. Never treat owner_telephone as a second phone. (VERIFIED, Groups A and E.)
- **TX TDLR owner_name equals business_name.** owner_name is the business name on Electrical Contractor rows, on all 71,435 cosmetology and barber establishment rows, and on all 4,768 Esthetician Establishment rows (a `owner_name != business_name` count returned 0). It is a licensee label, not a person. The same trap appears in Montgomery County MD hvxt-ztkq (owner field holds the business name). (VERIFIED, Groups A, D, E.)
- **CT active='1'.** Supplied by the caller's brief as a known filter trap on the Connecticut dataset; the CT DMV row (apne-w8c6) in the Group G module does not restate it, so treat it as REPORTED from the brief and confirm the active flag semantics before filtering.
- **MN phone reuse.** MN DLI CSVs reuse one phone across up to 189 licenses (answering services, license mills). Build a phone frequency table and exclude any phone attached to more than 2 distinct business names or more than 3 distinct persons from Bucket 2 and 3; keep the identity and send it to append. (VERIFIED earlier, Group A.)
- **CSLB status CLEAR.** Registers keep expired, suspended and out of business rows; filter CSLB to status CLEAR (Active elsewhere) with an expiration in the future, then require a live Maps listing or a permit in the last 12 months. (Group A.)
- **BatchData results unordered and 403 in body.** Supplied by the caller's brief: BatchData responses do not preserve input order (match on a request id or the input key, never on position) and can return an HTTP 200 with a 403 style error inside the response body, so parse the body status per record. The Group A and B modules cite BatchData pricing only and do not restate this behavior; treat it as REPORTED from the brief.
- **NPPES organization_name wildcard is trailing only.** `organization_name=*MED SPA*` behaves as a prefix match (leading `*` ignored); "contains" queries need the bulk file SQL. (VERIFIED, Group D.)
- **NPPES skip ceiling 1,200 rows.** `limit` max 200 (250 silently returns 200); `skip` max 1000, and `skip=1200` silently repeats `skip=1000`. Slice queries by 3 or 4 digit `postal_code` prefix with `address_purpose=LOCATION`, dedupe on `number`. (VERIFIED, Group C.)
- **NPPES state alone is refused** ("Field state requires additional search criteria"); `taxonomy_description` matches text on any taxonomy, so filter `taxonomies[].primary == true` client side; `city` without `address_purpose=LOCATION` matches mailing addresses. (VERIFIED, Group C.)
- **CMS datastore zip_code is 9 digits**; use the `starts with` operator. (VERIFIED, Group C.)
- **NY DMV nhjr-rpi2 expiration_date is text MM/DD/YYYY** and expired facilities up to 6 years old are included; the `like '%2027'` filter is the working active gate. (VERIFIED, Group G.)
- **NPPES AO phone differs bucket is polluted by HQ desks**: 64 percent of staff titled AO records carry a differing phone (Heartland 217-540-5xxx, Connect Hearing 630-303-5380, Irvine 714-845-8500). Apply the AO repetition, geography and title rules before any dial. (VERIFIED, Group C.)
- **FL DFS files carry Excel ="..." guards** and the Business file is served as binary (test UTF-16). (Group F.)
- **FL DBPR real estate CSVs have no header**; the last two positional fields are employer name and CQ number. (VERIFIED, Group F.)
- **SEC monthly zip names are irregular** (_0, _1, -registered suffixes); scrape the page for the href; send a descriptive User-Agent. (Group F.)
- **Socrata fetch limits.** URLs over roughly 300 characters were rejected and parenthesised OR lists were silently dropped on data.ny.gov and data.texas.gov; the PA instance returned 400 on aliased aggregates with "as". Use `$query` or POST bodies and an app token in production. (Groups B, E, G, H.)
- **NY OCFS phone_number_omitted Y and address_omitted Y** are the provider's opt out; do not append around them. (Group H.)
- **CA CHHS small family homes**: HSC 1596.86(b) restricts use; do not dial the CHHS phone for small homes. (Group H.)
